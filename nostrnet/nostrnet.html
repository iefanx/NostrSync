NostrNet Backup

This feature will download all your Nostr data from all the relays, create a copy, and broadcast it to all the Nostr relays. The copy will also be stored in the local database You can minimize this but don't close the NostrNet app.

Relay-to-Relay Sync

Sync your data across specific relays. This mode fetches all your events from the provided list and broadcasts them back to each one, ensuring your data is perfectly mirrored across all selected nodes.
